SURVIVE THE FIRE

01 MOVE & AIM

WASD or ARROWS to move. Aim with the mouse and LEFT CLICK to fire. SHIFT sprints; SPACE combat-slides.

02 LOOT & SURVIVE

Walk near glowing drops and press E. Swap between two weapons with 1 / 2 or the mouse wheel. Press H to heal and G to deploy a gloo wall.

03 BEAT THE STORM

The safe zone shrinks in stages. Stay inside the blue ring — the red storm drains health fast. Use buildings, trees and gloo walls for cover.

04 LAST ONE STANDING

There are 24 operators on the island. Eliminate rivals or let the storm do the work. When the smoke clears, only one name is left on the board.
